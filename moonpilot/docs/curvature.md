# Plan-timed path steering
The implementation and its three optional rows are in `moonpilot/curvature.py`.

- **The response horizon is `capture_age + lat_delay`.** `capture_age = now - model.timestampEof * 1e-9`, while `message_age = now - model_recv_time` independently measures how long the message sat after receipt. `controlsd` supplies its `selfdriveState` receive time as `now`, the `modelV2` receive time, and `lateralDelay + LAT_SMOOTH_SECONDS`; the fork reads `orientationRate.z` on the time grid published in `model.orientation.t`. On routes 413–418 the capture age was 35 / 60 / 81 ms (p5 / p50 / p95) at control time, so the freshness gate passed on every frame above 5 m/s.
- **The delay is the fork's estimate, learned down to 10 m/s, and applied under a cap.** Upstream `lagd` learned only above 50 mph and carried that one number to every speed: on an 810 s sub-50-mph route `lateralDelay` sat bit-identical at 0.220576, and routes 000003f3…0000040a show the mechanism — 28 cached blocks at a mean of 0.228 against ~0.40 s measured by the same estimator on those drives, because a highway block is rare and weighs 1/50. `moonpilot/lagd.py` now publishes `lateralDelay` with a 10 m/s gate, a 10-block ring and a cache that keeps its blocks; the per-band replay behind the gate read 0.40 / 0.36 s at 10–17 / 17–22 m/s (3 and 11 completed blocks; none completes above 22 m/s on those routes). Below 10 m/s too few windows pass the quality gates to say anything, so a parking-lot or intersection delay is still the cruising one. These are closed-loop numbers and they move with the controller in the wrong direction: the torque controller's feedback holds the wheel to the request from `lat_delay` ago, so the wheel's lag behind the request follows the applied delay (replayed above 10 m/s today, `python -m moonpilot.lab lagd-loop`: request→wheel 224 / 298 / 312 ms at the logged applied delays 0.228 / 0.320 / 0.320 s, model→wheel 232 / 319 / 384 ms), and the estimate reads that lag plus the vehicle's response — `0000040e`'s first partial block read 0.469 s at 0.32 applied. So the applied value is capped at `MOONPILOT_LAT_LAG_MAX_APPLIED` = 0.25 s while the blocks and `lateralDelayEstimate` stay uncapped and logged.
- Stale, future-dated, malformed, slow or out-of-grid input passes the request through bit-for-bit. A valid `lateralManeuverPlan` bypasses the fork reference because it has no matching `modelV2` trajectory.
- The module remains `curvature.py`, not `path.py`, because `moonpilot/paths.py` owns storage roots. `moonpilot/tests/test_curvature.py` pins the factory's bindings, the ramp lead of exactly `slope * tau`, the filter settling on the unfiltered ramp once led, the lateral-acceleration bound, and every freshness/shape pass-through against fixtures that are corrected when fresh.
- **`MoonpilotPathSmooth`** is an opt-in control-rate filter led by its selected time constant. On `0000040d` at 0.10 s and `0000040e` at 0.05 s, the filter delayed the request by 60 / 30 ms; adding τ to the torque setpoint delay would count that lag again. The replay results follow.

  | τ | lag, no lead | lag, lead τ | step RMS (unfiltered 8.5 mm/s² per frame) | rms deviation from the action, no lead → lead |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0.05 s | 34 ms | 18 ms | 3.8 → 4.0 | 21.5 → 19.5 mm/s² |
  | 0.10 s | 75 ms | 25 ms | 3.1 → 3.2 | 32.8 → 25.8 mm/s² |
  | 0.15 s | 117 ms | 26 ms | 2.7 → 2.9 | 43.7 → 31.5 mm/s² |

  `python -m moonpilot.lab smooth` reproduces the lag columns over the same four routes. The table used the cross-correlation peak. The lab instead uses the shift that minimizes the RMS difference over all pooled steering frames, and reads 40 / 83 / 124 ms without a lead and 13 / 20 / 23 ms with lead τ.

  The model replans faster than its own path predicts: 1.5–2 × τ lead reached ~0–11 ms lag but raised action deviation to 20.5–26.2 / 28.1–40.4 / 34.7–52.4 mm/s²; at 0.10–0.15 s lag spread went negative by tens of ms. On `00000408--9c6c861389`, per-frame P-term step RMS at <3 / 3–9 / ≥9 m/s fell from 169 / 65 / 13 CAN units to 68 / 33 / 7 at 0.05 s, 52 / 27 / 6 at 0.10 s and 49 / 25 / 6 at 0.15 s. Tests pin the filter and lead behavior.
